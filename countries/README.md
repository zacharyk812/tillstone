# Country presets

Each country or region gets its own folder here, bundling everything Tillstone needs to operate legally there:

- tax presets
- receipt rules (required fields, numbering, legal text)
- the fiscal adapter and its settings, if the country requires one
- the data retention period
- audit export formats

The design is described in [the plan, §15](../docs/PLAN.md#15-countries-fiscal-laws-and-tamper-proofing).

## Status levels

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Supported** | Preset complete, verified against current law, and tested |
| **Adapter in progress** | Work underway |
| **Available through a certified distribution** | The law requires a named party to certify or declare the software; a third party provides a certified build |
| **Not yet supported** | Nobody has started yet. Contributions welcome |

No countries are supported yet. If you know the tax or fiscal rules of your country well, please open an issue.
