# Governance

Tillstone is currently maintained by its founder, [zacharyk812](https://github.com/zacharyk812), who makes final decisions on the direction of the project.

## How decisions are made

- Day-to-day changes are decided in pull request review.
- Significant design decisions are proposed and recorded as [decision records](docs/adr/), so the reasoning stays visible.
- Anyone can propose a change to the plan or a decision record by opening an issue or pull request.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who make sustained, high-quality contributions may be invited to become maintainers. As the maintainer team grows, this document will be updated with a more formal process, such as consensus among maintainers.

## Certified distributions

In some countries, the law requires a named person or company to certify or declare a specific version of point-of-sale software (see [the plan, §15](docs/PLAN.md#15-countries-fiscal-laws-and-tamper-proofing)). Anyone may create such a certified distribution under the Apache License 2.0. The Tillstone project itself doesn't certify builds or take legal responsibility for them.
